Step 2 · her cards

Nothing issued yet. Fill the form and press Get her cards.

  1. Press Save her file. In the box that opens, choose Save as PDF, not a printer. That is her card, her birthday card and her kit card, behind a cover page that explains the rest.
  2. Then each gift card on its own. Pick it above, press Save just this card, repeat. One file per friend.
  3. Never send her the gift cards in one file with her own. She forwards what you send her, so one file would show a friend her balance and every other gift serial.
  4. Send them on the branch WhatsApp. PDFs, not photos: WhatsApp crushes an image and the serial is the first thing to go.
  5. Paste her name and her serial into Phorest with the buttons above, so the sale and the card match.
  6. Tell her to hand the friend cards out early. Their two months start today, not the day she gives them away.
  7. The kit card goes out in her file, but the kit waits on her Confidence Mapping. Her kit is matched to her there and cannot be made up before her answers are in the info@ inbox. The cover and the back of the card both say so, and the card carries the mapping QR, so point her at it when you send the file.
  8. The kit card is an allowance towards her Home Ritual Kit, never the kit itself. Total the kit at shelf value, take the allowance off, and say the difference before it is made up. She can ask for the fewest items that will work, and term 8 gives her that, so offer it rather than waiting to be asked.
