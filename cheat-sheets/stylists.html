Stylists and beauticians

Any client can now ask any one of you, so the risk is no longer who is on a list, it is who was briefed. This is the short version: what the voucher is, what her plan reaches, and the four things that must never come out of your mouth. Reception owns the numbers and the dates.

If you remember one sentence

“It is not money off. You place your credit, we add more on top, and it becomes your plan for the year.”

Printing for the staff room, or sending it to the group: the floor memo. Twelve pages of A4 at large print, and it carries the whole campaign rather than a summary of it: how it works in five steps, one client followed month by month, the prices, what each plan includes, this sheet's rules, and every term she agreed to when she paid. Page 1 on its own is enough to hand to a new starter, and there is a contents list on it so nobody has to hunt.

1What it actually is

She pays her own money in. We add extra credit on top. She spends it with us over the length of her plan.

Reception knows which plan she is on. Ask before you book her colour. There are three, running 6, 9 or 12 months. Which one she has decides how far up the colour ladder she reaches, and that is the only reason you need to know it.
She will call her plan by its name, not by a number of months. Dip Your Toes, Season of You, All-In VIP Year. If she names one, do not work out which is which in your head. Reception will tell you.

2Your commission

It is not taken away. It moves to when she spends the credit. You still get paid. Her voucher is credit sitting on her account, so the commission lands as she uses it rather than on the day she bought it.
If you are leaving before she has spent it, ask your branch manager. There is no answer written down for that yet, so nobody on the floor should be inventing one, including you.

3Four things you never say

  • “Free.” She paid for it herself.
  • “Unlimited.” The credit is a limit, not a free pass.
  • “Your kit is included.” Say towards. She always pays a difference.
  • Any number for colour. Colour is a ladder, not a budget. There is no amount to quote.

4Colour, check her plan first

The same three-step ladder you already use. Each plan reaches one step further, and includes everything beneath it.

StepBlondeBrunetteThe servicesReached by
1SoftPolished Root retouch and regrowth, all-over and single-process colour, toner, gloss and glaze, tonal maintenance All three plans
2SignatureDimensional Balayage and freehand placement, highlights and foils, grey blending, multi-tonal depth 9 and 12 month plans
3TransformationLuxury Full transformation, colour correction and removal, high lift, fantasy colour, big-contrast change 12 month plan only
Cuts, blow-dry, keratin and treatments are not on this ladder at all. They are covered on every plan. The ladder is about colour and nothing else.
She can always have a service above her step. She settles that one herself, outside her credit, in the usual way. It is not refused, it is not covered. Say it that way round, and say it before the appointment, never as a surprise at the till.
The one that will catch you out: her Self-Care Bonus never buys colour. On the 9 and 12 month plans the bonus is for beauty, treatments, keratin and blowdries only. It cannot go on her balayage.
If you cannot tell which step a service sits on, do not guess. Ask reception before she sits down. Then tell Kate, so the ladder gains the name rather than the same question coming back next week.

5Home kit, price before you pack

This is the one most likely to cause a scene, because it used to be a gift.

Say it like this, before the bag is packed“You have AED 450 towards your kit, and your products came to 910, so that is 460 today.”
Never say her kit is included. She has an allowance towards it, and she pays a difference at every plan. She hears the number before the products go in the bag, never after.
No Confidence Mapping and no payment means no kit. That is real policy, not a preference.
Her line, her six products, both emirates and what she pays: the Home Ritual Kit page.

6What the credit does not cover

  • Home care and any other product purchase
  • New hair extensions
Her kit allowance is separate from her credit. Two different pots. Her credit never pays for home care, and her kit allowance never pays for a service.
And it does not cross emirates. Whichever emirate she bought in is where her credit spends: Saadiyat and Khalifa City A, or Al Quoz and Motor City. So never offer a Dubai client a deep cleanse facial or LED, those are Abu Dhabi only, and her credit cannot travel there to pay for one.
Everything else is fair game, within her plan. Hair, colour on her step, treatments, keratin, blowdries, and beauty as far as your branch offers it.

7Her credit is safe, and her dates are not yours

Her credit is never at risk. Cancelling, rescheduling, arriving late or missing an appointment does not touch it. Say that first, because it is the actual worry underneath the question. Everything else follows the salon’s normal booking policy, unchanged.
Never quote her an end date. There is more than one date on her account and they are not the same on every plan. Reception owns dates. Send her there rather than being approximately right.

8After 30 September

Purchases close. Nothing else changes. Every rule on this sheet still applies for as long as her plan is running, which for some clients is well into 2027.

A client who bought in September has a live plan for months afterwards. Telling her in November that the voucher is finished is the same mistake as telling her it was free.
