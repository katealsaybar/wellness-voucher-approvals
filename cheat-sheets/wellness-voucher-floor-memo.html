Floor memo: the Wellness Voucher

Clients are already asking about this at the basin and at the till, and most of them have it slightly wrong. Read page one and you can hold your own with a client today. The rest is for when she asks something you have not heard before.
To  every stylist and beautician

Date  21 August 2026
Re  what to say when she asks
If you only remember one line

“It’s not money off. You put your own credit in, we add more on top, and it becomes your plan for the year.”

1 How it actually works

  • She pays us up front, in one go. Say AED 2,500.
  • We add money on top of what she paid. AED 500 on that plan, and she doesn’t pay for that part. She’s now got AED 3,000 sitting on her account.
  • She spends that AED 3,000 with us, one appointment at a time. Six, nine or twelve months, depending on which plan she bought.
  • Every visit, her services come off the balance. Like money in the bank. Nothing to pay at the till while there’s balance left.
  • Balance gone, or months up, and she’s back to paying normally.

So it isn’t free and it isn’t money off. She paid for most of it herself. We put a bit more on top because she paid up front, and because it books her in for the year instead of once. Nobody is asking you to sell this. Reception handles the selling, the cards and the dates. You just need to not say the wrong thing.

What it is not

Four things clients turn up assuming, and the six words she will say to you that you want to recognise straight away.
Not sure?
Ask before you tell her.
Reception first.

4 Four things she will assume

It’s not money off. She pays our normal menu price for every service. The only extra is the credit we added on top.
It’s not a membership. Nothing renews, nothing repeats. She buys it once, uses it, done.
It’s not points or a loyalty card. It’s her own money sitting on her account, waiting to be spent with us.
It’s not coming off today’s bill. It’s money she already paid us, spread across the next few months.

5 The words she will use

Her credit, or her balance. The money on her account, hers to spend on services with us. It’s a spending limit, not a free pass.
Her Self-Care Bonus. The extra we added on top, on the two bigger plans. Beauty, treatments, keratin and blowdries only. Never colour.
Confidence Mapping. The proper consultation where we write her plan and choose her products, before she’s in the chair. Every plan includes it, and she can’t collect her kit without it.
Her Home Ritual Kit. Her take-home products. She gets an amount towards it, not a free kit, and she pays the difference when she collects.
Dip Your Toes, Season of You, All-In VIP Year. The three plans. She’ll say the name, not the months. They run 6, 9 and 12 in that order.
Any client, any service, any stylist. There’s no list of who may take her booking and nothing is held back. She can move between you as often as she likes.

Where she can spend it

Her credit stays in the emirate she bought it in. Promising her a service your branch does not do is the easiest mistake on this campaign, and the one she takes personally.

10 Her credit doesn’t travel

There’s an Abu Dhabi voucher and a Dubai voucher. An Abu Dhabi voucher can’t be spent at Al Quoz or Motor City, and a Dubai voucher can’t be spent at Saadiyat or Khalifa City A.

If she thinks she bought the wrong one, send her to reception before she spends any of it, while we can still sort it out.

12 The three she will ask you

“Can I use it at your other salon?” Inside her own emirate, yes, either branch. Across emirates, no.
“Can my daughter use my credit?” The credit is hers alone. The AED 100 cards are the part she gives away.
“Can I book any stylist?” Yes. Any stylist, any service on your branch’s menu, as often as she likes.

Her home kit, and your commission

The kit is the one most likely to cause a scene at the till, because it used to be a gift and now it is an allowance. Your commission is the one you are all quietly wondering about, so it is at the bottom.
Say the number
before the bag is packed.
Never after.

13 Money towards the kit, not a free kit

She has an amount towards her products: AED 100 for 2 products on Dip Your Toes, AED 200 for 4 on Season of You, AED 450 for 6 on All-In VIP Year. She pays a difference on every plan.

Word for word “You’ve got AED 450 towards your kit, your products came to AED 910, so that’s AED 460 today.”

No kit leaves the salon until her Confidence Mapping is done and the difference is paid. That is policy, not a preference. If she would rather keep the bag small, she tells us before it is built, not after.

14 Two pots, and neither crosses over

Her service credit pays for services only.

Her kit money pays for home care only.

Her Self-Care Bonus is the beauty part of her credit: beauty, treatments, keratin and blowdries. Never colour.

  • Her credit never pays for home care, or any other product
  • Her credit never pays for new hair extensions
  • Neither pot ever comes back as cash, and neither is refundable

So an All-In VIP Year client has AED 4,500 for colour, not 5,400, and a Season of You client has AED 2,500, not 3,000. Everything else is fair game: hair, colour, keratin, treatments, blowdries and beauty.

15 Your commission

It is not being taken off you. It moves to when she spends the credit, because the voucher is money sitting on her account. You still get paid.

If you are leaving before she has spent it, ask your branch manager rather than working it out between yourselves.

What never to say

Four sentences that cost us money or trust, and what to say instead. This is not a telling off. Three of the four are things any of us would say without thinking.
“Let me check”
is the right answer,
not a weak one.

16 Four you never say

  • “Free.”She paid for it herself.
  • “Unlimited.”The credit is a spending limit, not a free pass.
  • “Your kit is included.”Say towards. She always pays a difference.
  • “Your bonus can go on your colour.”It cannot. Beauty, treatments, keratin and blowdries only.

17 What to say instead

  • “You put your credit in, we add more on top.”Instead of free. It is her money and she should get the credit for that.
  • “You’ve got AED 3,000 to spend with us.”Instead of unlimited. A real number lands better than a vague promise.
  • “You’ve got AED 450 towards your kit.”Instead of included. Then say what she pays today.
  • “Your credit covers your colour, your bonus is for beauty and treatments.”The one line that keeps the two pots straight.

18 When you don’t know

“Let me check that for you.” Then reception. If reception isn’t sure, your branch manager. Nobody guesses, and nobody gets it in the neck for asking.

A wrong answer on the floor costs us far more than a five-minute question, and it is the client who ends up disappointed. If the answer isn’t in this memo at all, tell Kate, so the next person doesn’t have to ask the same thing next week.

Cancelling, and her dates

The two she asks most once she has bought. The first has a reassuring answer you can give on the spot. The second is the one never to guess.

19 “What if I cancel?” Her credit is never at risk

Cancelling, rescheduling, running late, missing an appointment: none of it touches her balance. Say that first, because it is the real worry underneath the question.

Everything else follows our normal booking policy, unchanged. There is no special campaign rule and there never was.

20 “When does mine run out?” Never guess a date

21 After 31 October, only the buying stops

She can’t buy one any more. Every other rule in this memo still stands for as long as her plan is running, which for an October buyer is well into 2027.

Telling her in November that the voucher is finished is the same size of mistake as telling her it was free.
