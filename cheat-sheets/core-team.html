Which giveaway is the expensive one

The credit uplift costs the delivery cost of a service. The kit costs cash out of the door. Product leaves the building at trade cost whether she values it or not, which is why the kit became an allowance on 19 August: roughly AED 125, 225 and 300 saved per sale, and the top-up is new retail at full margin. A 200 allowance against a 534 basket is AED 334 of revenue that did not exist before, from a woman already at the till.

The floor, five things that cost money when they go wrong

  • Check her tier before booking the colour, never after. No cap, no sum: it is a ladder, see §5.
  • The Self-Care Bonus never buys colour. So a VIP Year client has AED 4,500 of colour headroom, not 5,400.
  • Tag Saadiyat clients as Saadiyat. They pay through the Khalifa City link, so without the tag her credit and the branch numbers land at the wrong salon.
  • Record who sold it. Attribution is how the branch numbers are read at the end of the window. If it is not recorded, it did not happen.
  • Tell her to hand the friend cards out early, and why. Two months from her purchase date, not from the day she gives one away.
Commission, for any stylist who asks“The voucher does not take your commission away, it moves it. When she comes back and spends her credit on a service, you earn your normal service commission on that visit, same as always.”

Flow rows stretch, they do not multiply. One week of rhythm exists and the window is six weeks. Repeating that week four times to make the grid look full is the one thing not to do: the calendar reports the hole instead. Add real weeks as rows with w: 2, w: 3 in data/calendar-data.js, then check the Mix panel rather than trusting the count.
Never type a number of days into a hook. A countdown row is anchored to the close and its number is a token worked out at render: {Days}, {n}, {closeDow}, {elapsed}. Tokens go in body text only, never in a title, because a note is pinned to a title. The window has already moved once.

Only Season of You moved on 19 August. The kit allowance came down from 250 to 200, which takes its headline from 3,900 to 3,850. Anything written before that date carries the old pair.
No colour cap, and no number to quote. The proposed AED 700 per visit was dropped, not numbered. Colour runs on the salon’s own three-step ladder and each voucher reaches one step further: Dip Your Toes to Soft and Polished; Season of You adds Signature and Dimensional, where balayage and highlights sit; All-In VIP Year adds Transformation and Luxury. Cuts, blowdries, keratin and treatments are not on the ladder at all and are covered on every tier. A service above her step is not refused, she settles that one herself.
Three expiry clocks, not one. The main voucher runs 6, 9 or 12 months from purchase. The AED 100 friend cards run two months from the buyer’s purchase date. Referral credit runs two months from the day the third new client has visited and paid, not booked. A line that says “two months” without saying from when is the one that generates the complaint.
Selected stylists was raised and closed on 20 August, both on the same day. Jumera asked for the promo to run through selected stylists only, carrying an earlier coordinators’ decision, and copy was written to it that morning. Tara settled it the same afternoon: it is a credit voucher, she spends it on any service, with any stylist, and she can move between them as long as the credit comes off her account. Nothing in client copy restricts who she sees, and the line that had been added was taken back out. What replaces the control is a briefing rather than a list: every branch, whole floor, before the blast sends. Recorded here so nobody reopens it in three weeks asking where it went.
Two vouchers, not one, and the branches are not interchangeable. Deep cleanse and clinical facials are Abu Dhabi only. Al Quoz does the full beauty menu plus the Signature Relaxing Facial. Motor City is hair only. Promising a facial in Dubai is the easiest mistake on this campaign.
Four words we never use, in copy or out loud: free (she placed the credit), unlimited (the credit is a spending cap at every tier), “your kit is included” (it is an allowance towards the kit and she always pays a difference), and any colour cap number. Also banned in client copy: discount in any form including negated, % off, premium, pamper, treat yourself, exclamation marks, the rose emoji, and em-dashes anywhere at all. British English throughout. “VIP” and “Ritual” are allowed on this campaign only, as the locked names.
