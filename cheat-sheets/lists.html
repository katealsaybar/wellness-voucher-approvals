The lists

Who actually gets contacted, how many of them there are, what was taken out of Belle’s export and why, and what each send costs. This is the sheet to quote from when someone asks for a number.

Wellness Voucher · purchases close 31 October 2026 · current as of 10 September 2026
If you remember one sentence

“2,910 women can be reached on WhatsApp and 1,631 by email, and the whole campaign costs AED 1,032 to send.”

The rows themselves are not on this site, and must not be put here. This page is on a public link. The cleaned files are client personal data: names, mobile numbers, email addresses, visit dates and lifetime spend for 3,218 real women. They live in Downloads\voucher-contact-lists-cleaned and are shared as files, direct to the person who needs them, never as a URL and never through a group chat. What belongs on this page is the counts and the method, which is all anyone needs to plan or to check the maths.

1One list, both channels

Decided by Kate, 19 August. It replaces the split agreed on 18 August.

Email and WhatsApp both go to Belle’s list and nothing else.

The 18 August call had sent email to the entire database and WhatsApp to the segment, on the grounds that email is nearly free. Belle spotted what that actually did: the top spender exclusion existed on WhatsApp only, so the women it removed were being sent the offer by email regardless. One list removes the contradiction, and there is one thing to export, import and de-duplicate instead of two.

Overlap is now total, and that is accepted. Everyone on the list gets both channels. At AED 0.0039 an email against AED 0.1709 a WhatsApp message, the cost of the overlap sits almost entirely on the WhatsApp side, where it was always going to sit.

3The 1,146 placeholder emails

The single biggest thing wrong with the raw export, and no validator would have caught it.

Where a client would not give an address, reception typed one in. Twelve distinct addresses account for 1,146 rows:

Sent as supplied, one real inbox receives 783 copies of the same campaign in a single run. Every one of these is syntactically valid, so a format check passes them. GHL has never sent a bulk blast, so the domain has no sending reputation to absorb that, and soc@gmail.com may well belong to a stranger who never heard of us.
Detection is by reuse, not by a list of known bad addresses. Any address shared by three or more different clients is treated as a placeholder. Run the same script on a future export and it catches whatever reception has started typing since, with no maintenance.
Nobody was dropped. These 1,146 women are held in a separate file and most of them are perfectly reachable on WhatsApp. They are a one-channel audience, not a loss.

WhatsApp is 96% of the bill. AED 0.1709 a message against AED 0.0039 an email, so a WhatsApp message costs 44 times what an email costs. Anything spent trimming the email side is spent on the wrong 4%. If the number has to come down, it comes down by sending one WhatsApp message instead of two, which is AED 497.26 on its own.
The wallet is the risk, not the budget. The GHL wallet holds AED 177 and this campaign needs AED 1,032. Auto recharge adds AED 184 whenever the balance drops below AED 37, so it would have to fire about five times. On 10 August it failed three times running before someone topped the wallet up by hand, and the 29 July subscription invoice failed three times before it cleared, so the card on file is not reliable. If the wallet empties mid send, the send stops and nothing raises an error. Pre-load AED 1,400 and check the card, before the send rather than during it.

7Two things about the export itself

Motor City is missing on purpose

Confirmed by Kate, 19 August. The export is correct as delivered and nobody needs to chase a file. Keep two things apart, because they will get conflated: who we contact excludes Motor City, but where the voucher works does not. The Dubai voucher is valid at Al Quoz and Motor City, and the landing page, the Stripe links and the tier table all say so. A Motor City client who hears about the campaign any other way can still buy and still redeem at her own branch. Do not tidy the copy by removing Motor City from the terms to match the contact list. The list is a targeting decision; the terms are a promise. Expect Dubai to be small by design, 286 against Abu Dhabi’s 2,624, and do not read that later as Dubai underperforming.

The Abu Dhabi branch names do not say which branch

The branch column carries two Abu Dhabi labels, and neither one names Saadiyat or Khalifa City. 1,501 rows read Tara Rose Hair and Beauty Salon and 1,375 read Tara Rose Hair and Beauty Salon - Turquoise 7. The emirate split is unaffected, so every number on this sheet stands. But nobody can segment Abu Dhabi by branch until Belle confirms which label is which, so do not guess it from the name.

8This is an active audience, not a database

All 3,218 women last visited in 2026. 730 in July, 603 in June, 603 in May, 425 in August, 381 in April. Median lifetime spend is AED 1,855, and 38% have spent over AED 2,500.

This settles the date-window question the 18 August call could not answer. There was a worry that a 2024 filter would produce a dormant list; what Belle actually pulled is the opposite, a current, high-value audience. Nothing here is dormant, so nothing needs the dormant messaging.

What was given up to get here. 14,818 contacts in GHL carry an email address, and email costs AED 0.0039 a send, so the reach dropped by going to one list was effectively free. Against that: the 14,818 is contacts-with-an-email, not customers, and much of it is people who enquired once and never came in. If the numbers come in short, the option is a second email-only send to the remainder after launch, rather than reopening the split now.
