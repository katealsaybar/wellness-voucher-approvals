How these sheets relate to the pack

The approval pack is the source of truth and carries the reasoning behind every line here. These sheets carry the answer without the reasoning, because a busy till and a build queue need the answer.

If a sheet and anything else disagree, the sheet is newer. Raise it with Kate rather than guessing, and the pack gets corrected rather than the sheet quietly diverging.
