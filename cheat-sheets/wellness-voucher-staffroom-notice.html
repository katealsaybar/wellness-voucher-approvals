If you only remember one line

“It’s not money off. You put your own credit in, we add more on top, and it becomes your plan for the year.”

How it works

  • She pays us up front, in one go. Say AED 2,500.
  • We add money on top. AED 500, and she doesn’t pay for that part. She’s got AED 3,000 on her account.
  • She spends it with us, one appointment at a time. Six, nine or twelve months, depending on her plan.
  • Every visit comes off the balance. Nothing to pay at the till while there’s balance left.
  • Balance gone or months up, she pays normally again.

Never say  /  say this instead

“Free.”
“You put your credit in, we add more on top.”
“Unlimited.”
“You’ve got AED 3,000 to spend with us.”
“Your kit is included.”
“You’ve got AED 450 towards your kit.” Then say what she pays today.
Any number for colour.
“Your credit covers colour, your bonus is for beauty and treatments.”
The three that catch everyone out
  • Her Self-Care Bonus never buys colour. Beauty, treatments, keratin and blowdries only. So an All-In VIP Year client has AED 4,500 for colour, not 5,400.
  • Her credit stays in the emirate she bought it in. Abu Dhabi credit can’t be spent at Al Quoz or Motor City, and Dubai credit can’t be spent at Saadiyat or Khalifa City A.
  • Her months start the day she paid. Not her first appointment, and not 31 October. Never guess her dates, only reception can quote those.
“Let me check that for you.”

Then reception. If reception isn’t sure, your branch manager. Nobody guesses, and nobody gets it in the neck for asking. A wrong answer on the floor costs us far more than a five-minute question.

The four that get quoted wrong
The AED 100 cards are for her friends, not for her. Two months from the day she paid, not from the day she gifts one. Tell her to hand them out early.
The home kit is money towards her products, not a free kit. She pays the difference when she collects, and nothing leaves until her Confidence Mapping is done.
The referral credit lands only once 3 friends have come in and paid. Not when they book. Then two months from that day.
Her birthday treat works any time inside her plan. Not just her birthday month. In Dubai it is the Signature Relaxing Facial at Al Quoz.
Reception does the cards, the dates and the serials.

You never have to. If she asks when something of hers runs out, send her to reception. There are four different dates on one client, and they each start from something different.
